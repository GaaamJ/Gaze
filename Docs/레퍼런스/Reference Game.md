# OneShot (2016, Future Cat, RPG Maker XP)
---
- **Protagonist**: Niko, catlike child in a sunless world, carrying a lightbulb sun to the top of a tower.
- **Player**: separate from Niko. The game addresses the player by their OS username. In-fiction the player is a "god" guiding the "messiah".
- **Meta**:
	- Quitting = Niko sleeps; reopening plays a dream of Niko's mother.
	- Puzzle answers live outside the game: Documents folder, desktop wallpaper, moving the window.
	- Inspired by Psycho Mantis (MGS) reading the memory card.
- **Ending**: Niko hands the final choice to the player.
	- Break the sun: world dies, Niko goes home.
	- Place the sun: world lives, Niko is trapped.
- **Second run (Solstice)**: world is a simulation reset; the previous run's memory feeds the story.


# Undertale (2015, Toby Fox)
---
- **Characters**: monsters, including Toriel, designed as "a mom that acts like a mom" and cares about the player's actions.
- **Player**: controls a child.
	- LOVE = Level of Violence, EXP = Execution Points.
	- Sans judges by those numbers.
- **Meta**:
	- The game remembers previous runs; boss dialogue changes on replay.
	- After a Genocide run the player must give up their soul, and the Pacifist ending is altered permanently. Resetting cannot erase it.
- **Genocide route**: the player kills monsters. Monsters evacuate and the remaining ones' dialogue changes.
- **Fox on art**: audiences attach more to simply drawn characters than detailed ones.


# Ib (2012, kouri, RPG Maker 2000)
---
- **Protagonist**: Ib, silent protagonist, little dialogue.
- **Player**: no meta. Controls Ib escaping a surreal gallery.
- **HP**: rose petals. Getting hurt drops petals.
- **Companions**:
	- Garry can die or go insane depending on the player's actions.
	- Mary looks like a girl Ib's age but is a painting. If her painting is not burned, she follows Ib out and lives as the second daughter.
- **Combat**: none, only evasion.
- **Influence**: The Witch's House, OMORI.


# Doki Doki Literature Club! (2017, Team Salvato, Ren'Py)
---
- **Characters**: Sayori, Yuri, Natsuki, Monika. Looks like a dating VN at first.
- **Events**:
	- Sayori confides depression, then hangs herself. The game ends and saves are erased.
	- Next run Sayori is gone and nobody remembers her.
	- Yuri self-harms, stabs herself; the player is forced to watch her body decay over a weekend.
- **Cause in-story**: Monika, a self-aware character, amplified the other girls' negative traits.
- **Meta**:
	- Monika confesses to the real player, not the protagonist.
	- The player must delete Monika's file from the game folder.
- **Reception** (critics, uncited): horror comes from loss of control and helplessness; the game "never turns its knives on you", it self-destructs instead.


# Depersonalization / 인격해체 (2022 EA, 2024 full, MeowNature)
---
- **What**: Chinese Call of Cthulhu–style TRPG game. Title means depersonalization (人格解体). Anthology of standalone scenarios, 2D exploration and text-adventure types.
- **Checks**:
	- Every check is a dice roll; roll under the target value to succeed (CoC d100).
	- On failure, spend Luck (a depleting resource) to reroll.
	- Success tiers exist ("hard success"); sanity is a resource.
- **Permanent marks**:
	- Scenario experiences become permanent positive or negative traits ("battered", "amnesia").
	- Traits carry into later scenarios, from CoC's rule that madness and remaining sanity persist.
	- An investigator cannot re-enter a cleared scenario.
	- A confirmed ending is stamped on the character forever; only deleting the character removes it.
- **Player**: the investigator is the player's avatar. Partners exist.


# Black Souls (2017, RPG Maker VX Ace)
---
- **What**: adult (R-18, can be toggled off) grim-fairy-tale RPG. Dark Souls systems + twisted fairy tales + Cthulhu. Lore spread over the whole game.
- **Characters**: many heroine companions taken from fairy tales and history (Little Red, Dorothy, Jeanne, Elma the match girl, Victoria, Goose, Catharina, Miranda, Elizabeth Báthory, Leaf the fairy).
- **Progress and deaths**:
	- As progress advances, NPCs die or turn into beasts (魔獣化). The dead never return. By the late game, most non-companions are dead.
	- Many heroines break if the player neglects them or does not act in time. Leave Goose chained, she becomes a beast; leave Elma home too long, she becomes a beast and burns the house; ignore Victoria, she kills herself.
	- Karma: raising it (assaulting characters etc.) makes Jeanne turn hostile and later become a beast; Catharina kills herself if spoken to at high karma.
	- Holding a certain ring from Leaf at a companion ending turns it into a bad ending where the heroine dies.
- **Player's options**: the player can assault, imprison, or kill heroines. Described as picaresque.
- **Meta**:
	- Opening line from Alice: "You are the protagonist of this story, so please never break until the very end."
	- Entering a specific name (the protagonist's true name) at name entry triggers an easter egg; Alice asks "did you remember?", then gets killed and the memory wiped.
	- Final boss Mary Sue (メアリ・スー), named after the author self-insert trope, created the world as a model garden and hid as companion Leaf.
	- The protagonist is revealed as the fairy-tale author himself (Grimm; also called Carroll and Andersen).
- **Endings**: 4 main (A–D) plus one per companion.


# Black Souls II (2018, same developer)
---
- **What**: sequel set in Wonderland / Through the Looking-Glass, influenced by American McGee's Alice. More Cthulhu, horror spots (jump scares, creepy BGM). Three large free DLCs that rework the game.
- **Goal**: find Alice. At the start the player picks who Alice is to them: daughter, sister, or mother. The ending's Alice changes accordingly.
- **SAN value (sen値)**:
	- Starts at 100. Killing NPCs, black souls, looking at "the girl's photo" lower it. Sex with heroines, items, eating corpses raise it.
	- ≤70: BGM disappears. ≤30: maps change, most NPCs become unintelligible. ≤0: heroines turn into monsters or show someone unexpected; strong enemies spawn.
	- Heroines and signs change dialogue with SAN (e.g. the Queen of Hearts' sign turns yandere).
	- The madness item can set SAN to 0 or 100 at will.
- **Progress and deaths**:
	- Jubjub, a bird spreading madness, is the main cause of heroine deaths. If not killed early, at progress 10 most characters kill themselves or die.
	- Individual collapses: twins where one kills the other then turns into a beast from guilt; a doctor who goes mad seeking immortality; a heroine who hangs herself if left lonely.
- **Oaths**: bond with heroines for buffs; killing or assaulting them cuts the oath.
- **Dice**: one boss reveals her true identity only if a die roll comes up 6.
- **Endings**: A–H. SAN sign decides between some (kill Alice at SAN+ → D, SAN− → E). A third of endings are reached by losing.
